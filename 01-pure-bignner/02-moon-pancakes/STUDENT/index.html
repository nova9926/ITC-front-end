Moon Pancakes 🌙

The easiest breakfast in the galaxy!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 spoon of sugar

How to make them

  1. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Pour a little mix into a warm pan.
  3. Cook both sides until golden.
  4. Add your favorite topping!

Chef's tip : Add a banana for extra happiness.

enjoy your pancakes!
